A little time for a good puzzle.

Some days you want a quick Easy. Other days that one 7 just will not reveal itself. Sudokura gives you a clean, welcoming Sudoku board, with the right tools close at hand and no complicated setup.

FOUR WAYS TO PLAY
• Classic: settle into a puzzle at your own pace.
• Strikes: three mistakes and the game is over. Choose wisely.
• Time Attack: race the clock, one square at a time.
• Daily Puzzle: a dated challenge to come back to each day.

Pick Easy, Medium or Hard. Add pencil notes, Undo and Redo, or use Hint and Verify when you want a little help. Switch between light and dark themes, with optional music and sound effects.

YOUR PUZZLES HAVE A STORY
Save progress in Saved Games and continue when you're ready. Your journey remembers solved puzzles, results and earlier attempts. Lost or interrupted a game? Try again brings back the same puzzle without erasing what came before.

Made for touchscreens, in portrait or landscape, and available in English, Español and Català.

YOUR GAME, YOUR DEVICE
Sudokura has no accounts, ads or analytics. Puzzles and history stay on your device; cloud sync, automatic Android backup and device transfer aren't provided.

Requires Android 9+ on ARM64. Keep any installation containing saved progress: app signatures can differ between distribution channels, so switching stores may prevent an in-place update. Free and open source (GPLv3).
